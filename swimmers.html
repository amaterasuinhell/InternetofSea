Swimmer gallery

These are the decorations that swim around the sea. They aren’t links. Each one is drawn from a few lines of code onto a 128×128 pixel canvas, and each has two frames that the sea swaps between to make it look like it’s swimming.

  1. Draw the body parts with a thick white stroke first, then the color on top. That creates the neon outline.
  2. Draw it twice, with one small change (tentacles curl the other way, the tail flips, the wings flap).
  3. In the sea, the picture is placed on a flat sprite that always faces you. The two frames swap with each pulse, and the sprite squashes and stretches a little.
  4. Each swimmer drifts in a direction, surges forward on each pulse, and slowly turns.
